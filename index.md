# MHH Bioinformatics Hematology Lab

The **MHH Bioinformatics Hematology Lab** is part of **AG Heidel** at **Hannover Medical School (MHH)**.

## What we do

We develop and maintain computational methods for genomics and chromosome conformation research, with a focus on high performance, robustness, and practical use in biomedical workflows.

## Agentic AI for faster and lighter genomics software

A core direction in our lab is an **agentic AI approach** to transform existing genomics tools for:

- higher speed
- lower memory and compute usage
- improved scalability on real-world datasets

Examples include optimization work around tools such as **VarDictCpp** and **FalcoAmadeus**, where we aim to modernize and streamline implementations while preserving scientific correctness.

## HiCExplorer maintenance

We actively maintain **HiCExplorer**, supporting reliable analysis workflows for Hi-C and related chromosome conformation data.

Our maintenance work includes keeping the software usable for the community, improving performance and compatibility, and supporting continued development.

## HiCGAN prediction project

We are also developing **HiCGAN** as a prediction-focused project, exploring machine-learning-based approaches to infer and enhance genomic interaction patterns from available data.
